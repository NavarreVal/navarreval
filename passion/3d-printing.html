Form 1 suppressor

I have a custom AR pistol chambered in .300 Blackout. I filed a Form 1 and paid the tax stamp so I could 3d Print a silencer. It actually worked really well!

The suppressor was printed in PET-CF, which required a hardened steel hotend. It is wrapped in a carbon fiber weave fastened with hardened epoxy resin.
