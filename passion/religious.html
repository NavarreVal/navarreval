A word of belief

Draft personal copy — not institutional teaching

I am a disciple of Jesus Christ. I try to hear Him in scripture, keep His commandments, and let His grace do the slow work of changing me. That is the center of my faith, and the reason this page exists.

I trust the restored gospel of Jesus Christ: that God still speaks, that priesthood authority was restored, and that His Church is on the earth again. I belong to it as a Latter-day Saint. What I write here is my own attempt to live that faith.

These words are personal. They are not a lesson manual, and they are not a substitute for the scriptures or for the living voice of the Church.
