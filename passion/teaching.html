Teaching

Goal

For a long time I led work in tech — Agile teams, projects, the daily job of getting people pointed the same way. I’m transitioning that into classroom teaching.

I’m pursuing teaching in Utah public schools. The place I picture is Iron County, and a school like Cedar Middle School: a bell, a hallway, a room where you can learn the students’ names and mean it.

The goal is to help students learn, grow, and find purpose. I want lessons that are clear, a room that stays warm when the work gets hard, and enough expectation that a young person can start to see what they are for. The point is the student.
