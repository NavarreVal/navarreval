About

Hi, I’m Navarre. I’m a husband, a hands-on dad, and someone who has spent the better part of a decade leading teams and delivering complex projects across fintech, healthcare, and blockchain.

After years as a Senior Scrum Master and Project Manager, I made a deliberate shift toward education. I’m currently pursuing a Master’s in Secondary Social Studies Education and preparing to teach high school. The decision was driven by a long-standing pull toward teaching, a strong family legacy in education, and a desire for work that feels more aligned with who I am.

I care about clear thinking, practical problem-solving, and building things that last — whether that’s high-performing teams, reliable systems, or a simple, well-made website. Outside of work you’ll usually find me spending time with my family, working on home projects, reading, or exploring Southern Utah.
